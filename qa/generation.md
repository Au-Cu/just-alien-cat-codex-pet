# Artwork history

The initial artwork was generated with imagegen, adapting the JUST Big Cat atlas. The current edition uses deterministic per-frame facial edits: slightly elongated eyes, no mouth, and separate nose and mole dots. Both the outline and color regions have been reconstructed with supersampling to reduce blur and color halos. The atlas remains 1536 × 2288 with 74 occupied frames.

Historical first-generation artwork is retained in preview/selected-first-version.png. Current appearance is shown in preview/base-preview.png and preview/contact-sheet.png.
